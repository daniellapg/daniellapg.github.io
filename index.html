POLITICAL AND POLICY ANALYST

I'm a final-year BA International Relations student at King's College London with a particular interest in security, humanitarian, and economic policy across Europe, the Middle East, and Latin America. This portfolio brings together four independent research pieces written in different professional formats to reflect both the range of my policy interests and my ability to write for different audiences. I'm particularly drawn to work that sits at the intersection of political analysis and real-world decision-making, whether in government, international organisations, or risk advisory.
